TL;DR

Stuff that isn't mine: if it's a collab, client work, or something I only contributed to, go check that project. it has its own rules and I don't speak for it.

Stuff that is mine: 99% of the time it's CC0. take it, remix it, print it on a mug, put it in your game. credit is nice but I'm not gonna chase you for it.

the other 1% will be clearly marked. if it isn't marked, assume CC0.

that said, there are four things I'm not okay with, CC0 or not:

  • AI. don't feed my work into training sets, datasets, fine-tunes, LoRAs, whatever. no.
  • Politically specific stuff. don't use my work to push a party, campaign, candidate, or hot-button political cause. same goes for things you know are 50/50, or where you're just trying to rage bait people.
  • Pure NSFW. don't turn my work into, or use it inside, straight-up porn.
  • Non-transformative re-uploads. don't just re-upload my stuff as-is somewhere else. do something with it first.

that's it. genuinely. everything else is fair game.

heads up: if I catch people trying to weasel around these four, I'll be forced to get way more restrictive with everything. don't ruin it for everyone else.
